What We Trade

We specialise in wholesale and retail trading of textiles, clothing and footwear, serving stalls and markets across Dhanbad district.

Textiles

Fabric by the metre for tailoring, home use and resale to local stall owners. Available in a variety of qualities and patterns.

Clothing

Ready-made garments for men, women and children. Sourced for market and stall trade. Daily wear, ethnic and contemporary styles.

Footwear

Everyday footwear stocked for retail sale across local markets and stalls. Durable and comfortable options for the whole family.

Our trading activity is focused on supplying stall owners and market vendors across Dhanbad district. We take pride in consistent quality and reliable sourcing.
